Speaking your moves

Turn the mic on (button or M), or hold Space in push‑to‑talk mode, then say a move:

  • “e four” · “echo four” · “pawn to e four”
  • “knight f three” · “knight takes e five”
  • “e takes d five” · “bishop takes c six check”
  • “knight b d two” · “rook on a one to d one”
  • “castle short” · “long castle”
  • “e eight queen” · “g one f three” (from‑to always works)

Commands: “repeat”, “time”, “offer draw”, “accept draw”, “decline draw”, “abort”, “resign”, “flip board”, “help”.

After a game say “review”, then “next”, “back”, “go to move twelve”, “next mistake”, “best move” or “summary”.

Only legal moves are listened for, so recognition stays accurate. Letters can be said plainly or with the NATO alphabet (alpha, bravo, charlie…).
